# MacStats

Five menu bar items for a Mac, CPU usage, GPU utilization, RAM in use, the temperature of the
hottest part and free disk space as Finder counts it, each with a panel of charts and top
processes that says what the number means.

All five update every second for well under 1% of one core, and it asks for no permissions. It
builds on your own Mac with one command, or installs from Homebrew.
