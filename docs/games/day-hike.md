# Day Hike

A first-person co-op horror game for up to five players, in the browser with no install, and the
project I have pushed AI-assisted development furthest on: nearly every one of its 1,800+ commits
was written with Claude Code, from the deterministic simulation and the WebRTC netcode to the
procedural world and the shaders.

Before building a system I research it with Claude, from how AAA games render ground fog and rain
to how horror directors build dread, and [publish what I find](/research). More than twenty of
those docs shaped what you see on the trail.
