# Who is standing in my hallway?

A 16 second analog horror short made from AI generated stills of one woman and one hallway, cut
apart and animated across frames, then finished with a VHS pass.

It tests a question I first [researched with Claude](/research/ai-short-film-generation): how far AI
generation can go toward a film that keeps the same actor and the same room from shot to shot.
