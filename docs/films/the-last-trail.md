# The Last Trail

A 56 second horror short about grief. A woman carries her late father's ashes up his old trail,
and on his ranger radio a voice that sounds like him starts giving her directions.

Made with AI, most of its shots with Veo 3.1. The story and the camera follow research I did into
[how horror directors build dread shot by shot](/research/horror-cinematography-shot-by-shot).
